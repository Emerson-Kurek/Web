01

Monolith

The straight read of the reference. Stone dead-centre, beam falling full height, two-thirds of the frame given to black. Price and promo sit under the plinth so the stone acts as a shelf for the offer.

Strongest when it can be seen from distance — the negative space is what makes it carry across a shop floor.

Prompt delta: baseline. Single overhead key, 45° cone, no fill.

02

Off-axis

Plinth and beam pushed to the right third; the copy column drops into the cleared left side. The offer reads first, the object second — closer to how a shopper scans a poster at arm's length.

Handles long product names and multi-line promos without crowding the stone.

Prompt delta: key light offset 30° right, stone at right third, floor pool follows the light.

A1 portraitMulti-buy / bundle
03

Double plinth

Two slabs stacked in the same column of light, the upper one smaller and dimmer. Built for multi-buy: one plinth per item, the mechanic sitting in the gap between them.

The only direction where the staging carries an actual message — don't use it for a single-product drop.

Prompt delta: two stone discs on one vertical axis, upper disc 60% scale, shared beam, two shadows at different densities.

04

Shelf edge

The landscape crop, for shelf talkers, gondola headers and till-point strips. Stone shrinks to a marker at the left, and the price block anchors the right where the eye exits.

Same lighting set-up as 01, re-framed rather than re-shot — so it can ship as a family with whichever portrait direction you pick.

Prompt delta: 16:5 crop, stone at left quarter, beam clipped by the top edge, floor pool widened.

05

Daylight

The negative of the set: bright bone-white ground, dark stone, shadow reduced to a soft grey pool. Nothing about the staging changes — only which side of the contrast the stone sits on.

The practical argument for it: black floods eat ink and go grey under shop fluorescents. This one holds at 60% coverage and survives a sun-facing window.

Prompt delta: invert ground to bone white, stone in dark basalt, diffuse overhead key, shadow at 20% density.

Fields on every poster

  • Brand or department line
  • Product name
  • One line of product copy
  • Price, with optional was-price
  • Promo mechanic
  • SKU and offer end date

What stays fixed

  • Single overhead key, no fill light
  • Raw unpolished stone, chiselled edge
  • Visible gap between stone and shadow
  • Greyscale only — no colour cast
  • Seamless ground, no horizon line
